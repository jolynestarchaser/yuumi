# All-species concept generation

Built-in imagegen, 2026-10-02. Each sheet inspected for six readable variants,
retained identity and full-body framing. These sheets are visual references;
the production SVG rigs are simpler, front-facing and deliberately retain the
existing animation/cosmetic system. They are not exact raster-to-vector copies.
Robot reference remains at ../robot-concept-v1.png with its prompt in ../prompts.md.

## cat-concept-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: cozy virtual companion cat evolution reference sheet. Seven full-body front-facing figures on plain warm ivory background: small BASE at top, then 3 rows of 2 figures. Rows are NATURE, CELESTIAL, ADVENTURER respectively; left COMPACT rooted rounded body and right AGILE elongated body. Species directions in row order: cream woodland feline with leaf cheek tufts; sleek moon feline with crescent tail; sturdy scout feline with broad paws. All figures remain recognizably the SAME original species, tiny dark eyes, gentle smile, pink cheeks, thick rounded dark brown outlines, simple flat pastel vector-like illustration, no pixel art. Compact and agile need genuinely different head and body contours and native limb placement, NOT mere recolors or costume swaps. Full body with generous margins, no overlap or cropping. Readable exact labels BASE, NATURE COMPACT, NATURE AGILE, CELESTIAL COMPACT, CELESTIAL AGILE, ADVENTURER COMPACT, ADVENTURER AGILE. No UI, watermark, or background scene. This is concept reference, not a spritesheet.

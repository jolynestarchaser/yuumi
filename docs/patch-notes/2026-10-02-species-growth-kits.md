# Companion walking and growth-art kits

- All 11 companions now have species-specific walking, hopping, waddling,
  marching, or drifting motion during desktop travel.
- Age affects body proportions and walking cadence independently of XP.
  Elder details preserve each species' identity, including a mechanical robot badge.
- Species-native evolution art replaces shared wings: membranes for dragons,
  feathers for ducks, petals for bunnies, leaves for spirits, fins for robots,
  and glider capes for storybook companions.
- Robot cables, grippers, and sensor arrays replace animal-like parts.
- Optional outfits, headwear, and hair are saved separately from earned anatomy.
- Reduced motion and animation-off remain supported. Sleep and care interrupt travel.
- No new level cap, equipment economy, age gates, or gameplay rollout is enabled.

Design reference: `docs/art-direction/species-age-evolution-kits.md`.
